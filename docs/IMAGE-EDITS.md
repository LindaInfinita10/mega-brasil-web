## Prompt de sustitución de rostros (cuatro fotos)

Use case: precise-object-edit. Edit target: the attached factory photograph for the Mega Brasil website. Replace ONLY the blurred faces of BOTH workers with natural, photorealistic, entirely fictional adult male faces, not recognizable real people. Fit each face precisely to the existing head angle, gaze, skin tone, scale and lighting, with natural detailed facial anatomy and no blurring. Keep the workers focused on their existing activity. Preserve everything else as closely as possible: exact portrait framing/aspect ratio, camera perspective, head positions, hard hats, safety glasses where present, uniforms, logos and lettering, hands, bodies, machinery, metal sheets, floor markings, architecture, lighting and colors. Do not add people, objects or text. Output the complete edited photo, not a collage.

## Prompt de versión horizontal

Use case: precise-object-edit. Input is the edit target: Mega Brasil factory photo already edited with fictional workers. Create a high-resolution horizontal 16:9 website hero version, 3840x2160 pixels. Expand the photo naturally to the left and right to show more of the SAME factory, preserving the existing central scene, existing fictional faces, red uniforms, workers, machinery, metal doors, yellow floor lines and exact sign text/logo 'MEGA BRASIL INDÚSTRIA'. Keep both workers visible. Photorealistic consistent architecture and perspective, sharp natural industrial detail suitable for a 4K TV. No additional people, signage or objects unrelated to the factory. No blur, no invented text, no collage, no borders. Left side should remain visually quiet for overlaying website headline text.
